# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Homeowners and property owners in and around Freudenberg, Germany, aged 35–65. They are comparing 3–5 local/regional contractors before making a significant outdoor investment (3.000–15.000€+). They arrive via Google search, word-of-mouth, or Instagram. Primary job: gain enough confidence in Monpro's quality and competence to request a quote. Secondary job: understand which products exist and whether the company can handle their specific situation.

## Product Purpose

A lead-generation website for Monpro Freudenberg, a premium aluminum terrace cover installer. The site exists to convert regional search traffic into quote requests. Success = a filled contact form or a phone call. It must project the quality and precision of the physical product despite being a low-budget build. The site must feel like an architectural firm's portfolio, not a generic contractor listing.

## Brand Personality

Competent, Industrial, Premium. The brand is self-assured — it does not need to over-explain. Like a skilled craftsman's workshop: clean, organized, and evidently capable. Voice is direct and confident without being cold. German engineering ethos without the corporate distance.

## Anti-references

- **DL Metallform style** (dl-metallform.de): template-heavy, generic card grids, star rating hero sections, cluttered founder-bio layout. Avoid.
- **Flashy 3D configurators**: out of scope and out of budget. No configurator links.
- **Weinor corporate tone**: too lifestyle-marketing, too aspirational for a regional craftsman. Avoid generic "Draußen zuhause" lifestyle copy.
- **Generic card grids**: product pages must not be a 3-column icon-card repeat. Use editorial layout instead.
- **Gradient text, glassmorphism, bounce animations**: none.

## Design Principles

1. **Craft over decoration.** Every visual detail should feel intentional. Whitespace is not emptiness — it signals precision.
2. **Show the work.** The portfolio/project gallery is the primary trust signal. It must dominate without clutter.
3. **Confident simplicity.** A single strong typographic hierarchy beats five UI flourishes. Less copy, more weight.
4. **Regional authority.** The site should make visitors feel Monpro is the obvious choice in Freudenberg — not just another online contractor.
5. **Budget discipline.** Every design decision must be achievable with HTML/CSS on a WordPress Tailwind theme. No design element that requires a paid plugin, a 3D configurator, or custom illustration.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum. Body text at minimum 16px. Focus states visible. Alt text on all project images. Contact form keyboard-navigable.

## Tech Delivery

WordPress hosted on Ionos. Custom lightweight theme built on Tailwind CSS v3. No page builder (Elementor, Divi). Custom post type for portfolio/projects managed via ACF Lite or native custom fields. Contact Form 7 for the inquiry form. Target: fast, no bloat, handoff-ready for a non-technical client.
